What is CuteFela?

An environmental organisation working across conservation, education, livelihoods and rights while connecting practical action with international environmental processes.

Vision and mission

Environmental work makes more sense when the pieces stay connected.

Our vision is to unite people to protect people and wildlife. Our mission is to nurture sustainability so wildlife thrives and the connection between people and the natural world becomes inseparable.

Our work

Different problems. One connected environmental reality.

Education, wildlife care and disaster response often overlap in the same places. Each programme has a clear purpose while still supporting the others where that connection is useful.

01 / School programs

Learn it. See it. Keep it going.

Environmental and rights learning supported by field exposure, student working groups and projects that can continue after a single session.

Explore school programs
02 / Wildlife rehabilitation

Recovery needs more than rescue.

Intake, diagnostics, treatment, suitable housing, rehabilitation, translocation and release all depend on the facilities and people that make each stage possible.

Explore rehabilitation
03 / Disaster response

Emergency help, recovery and resilience.

A three stage approach covering immediate relief, reconstruction and rehabilitation, and the longer work of helping communities prepare for future hazards.

Explore disaster response
