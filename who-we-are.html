Who we are

People and wildlife belong in the same future.

CuteFela connects biodiversity, environmental learning, livelihoods and rights while keeping practical work close to the people, species and places it is meant to serve.

Our direction

Global in outlook. Grounded in practical work.

CuteFela began in Sri Lanka and works from the premise that environmental problems rarely fit inside a single category. Wildlife loss can be tied to livelihoods. Climate hazards can become rights issues. Education matters more when students can connect ideas to real places and institutions.

That is why our work moves between field activity, environmental learning, public interest thinking and international processes. Each part has a different purpose, but they can support one another when the connection is useful.

VisionUnite people to protect people and wildlife.
MissionNurture sustainability so wildlife thrives and the people nature connection becomes inseparable.
Home baseSri Lanka, with a global environmental outlook.
How we think

Evidence first. Relationships that lead to useful work.

We aim to communicate clearly about what the organisation does, where the work comes from and which sources support important claims. Real programme media and institutional sources are prioritised wherever they are available.
